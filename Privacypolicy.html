Privacy policy Tamoveer · Nitai Studio · Effective 16 October 2026 Tamoveer is an offline fantasy action game developed by Nitai Studio. This policy describes the code included in version 2.0.0. Game data stays on your device The game stores your selected warrior, settings, scores, mastery, campaign stars, upgrades and daily challenge progress in local storage. This data is not sent to Nitai Studio. You can erase it using Settings → Reset Local Progress, or by clearing the app's storage. Uninstalling may also remove it. No accounts, ads or analytics This version includes no account system, advertisements, tracking SDKs, analytics, in-app purchases or cloud saves. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or advertising permissions. The Android app bundles its game assets and works without an internet connection. Web version If you play the web version on a hosted website, your browser downloads the game files and may cache them for offline use. The hosting provider can receive normal connection information, such as your IP address, while serving those files. That provider's privacy policy applies to its hosting logs. The game code itself sends no telemetry. Contacting support The support button opens your email application. If you choose to email us, we receive the information you send and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal details. Children and changes The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
